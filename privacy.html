Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026
The short version: Tiltwise has no accounts and sends me no personal information. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple. The optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Tiltwise is made by Max Trilling ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the Tiltwise iPhone app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, cosmetics and purchase records are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to me or to any server I run. I don't have one.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. I never see your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (optional reward videos)

Tiltwise offers optional videos that you can choose to watch in exchange for hints, gems and other in-game rewards. They are provided by Google AdMob. When a video is requested, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about ad views and interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ads.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Tiltwise may track you. You can change your answer any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The game works exactly the same either way.
  • Consent: Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), Google's consent message asks for your choice first. You can change it in Tiltwise Settings → Videos → Ad privacy choices.
  • No videos at all: Turn off Settings → Videos → Optional video offers. No ads are requested while offers are off.

Children

Tiltwise is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything Tiltwise stores lives on your device. Use Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app, to remove it. To reset Google's advertising data, see Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
