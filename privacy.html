Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026
The short version: Tiltwise has no accounts and sends me no personal information. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ads (optional reward videos, and one short ad every 10 levels) are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them, as described below. I don't sell your personal information.

Tiltwise is made by Max Trilling ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the Tiltwise iPhone app and this website handle, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, cosmetics, gem and hint balances, and purchase records are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage (included in your normal iPhone backups). They are never sent to me or to any server I run. I don't have one. Google's ad SDK also saves your ad consent choice on your iPhone.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. I never see your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Advertising

Tiltwise shows two kinds of ads, both provided by Google AdMob:

  • Optional reward videos that you choose to watch in exchange for hints, gems and other in-game rewards. They only play when you tap a button that offers one.
  • A short ad between levels: one ad after every 10 levels, starting at level 20, shown when you tap Next. It never interrupts a level. The No-Video Pass purchase removes it.

No ads are requested during the first few levels. After that, when an ad is requested, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about ad views and interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ads.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. If you allow tracking (and, where required, give consent), Google may also use it to personalize ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Tiltwise is rated 4+, so it only asks Google for ads suitable for general audiences.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Tiltwise may track you. You can change your answer any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The game works exactly the same either way.
  • Consent and opt-out: Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws), Google's privacy message asks for your choice or lets you opt out. You can change it any time in Tiltwise Settings → Videos → Ad privacy choices.
  • Fewer ads: Turning off Settings → Videos → Optional video offers hides every reward video offer. The short ad every 10 levels still plays unless you have the No-Video Pass.

Your US state privacy rights

I don't sell personal information for money, and Tiltwise collects no personal information itself. Under some state laws (such as California's), letting Google use device data for personalized ads can count as "sharing" personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out of that by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" when iOS asks (or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), and where available, in Tiltwise Settings → Videos → Ad privacy choices.

You also have the right to ask what personal information I hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because Tiltwise sends me nothing, I hold none, but you can email me with any request and I'll answer within 45 days. For data Google holds, use Google's My Ad Center or the links above. You won't be treated differently for using any of these rights.

Do Not Track

The app doesn't use web browsers, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't apply to it. Use the iOS tracking setting above instead. This website doesn't track you at all.

This website

This website has no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Tiltwise is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you're a parent or guardian and have a question or request about your child, email me.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress erases your progress, cosmetics, gems and settings on that iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything Tiltwise stored, including Google's saved consent choice. One-time purchases (Starter Pack, No-Video Pass) can be restored afterwards from the Gem Store. To reset the Advertising Identifier, use iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects what information is collected or shared, the app's App Store privacy details will be updated too.

Contact
