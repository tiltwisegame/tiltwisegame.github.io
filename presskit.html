Tiltwise press kit

Tilt. Roll. Plop.

Tiltwise is a calm, clever marble puzzle game for iPhone. Swipe to tilt the board: every marble rolls until it hits something. Get each one into the hole of its own color in as few tilts as you can.

Description

Each Tiltwise level is a little machine. Marbles block marbles, crates slide into pits, and the right tilt at the right moment makes everything drop into place with a satisfying plop. It's easy to learn, there are no lives, energy or timers on regular levels, and a hint always knows the best next move from wherever you are.

Features

  • 400 hand-checked levels across 20 themed worlds, each ending in a boss level, then fresh levels that never run out
  • 12 mechanics: crates, pits, sticky pads, one-way arrows, portals, gates, paint splats, bumpers, cracked tiles and twin marbles
  • Three new daily puzzles (easy, medium, hard) with a streak and a spoiler-free result to share
  • Rush, a timed mode, and Zen, small relaxing boards with no stars and no pressure
  • Over 180 cosmetics: ball textures, designs, hats, trails, board themes, music styles and sound packs
  • Color-blind assist, a left-handed layout, reduced motion, and undo for everything
