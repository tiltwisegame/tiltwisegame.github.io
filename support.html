Help & support

Tiltwise for iPhone
Need help? Email tiltwise.help@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within a couple of days.

How do I play?

Swipe in any direction to tilt the board. Every marble rolls until it hits a wall, a crate or another marble. Get each marble into the hole of its own color. Solving in the target number of tilts (par) earns ★★★.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap the light bulb for a hint. It always shows the best next move from where you are. You earn free hints from stars, daily puzzles and bosses. Undo takes back any move, and tapping Undo right after Restart brings your moves back.

My purchase didn't arrive / I got a new phone

Open the Gem Store (tap your gem count on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. The Starter Pack and the No-Video Pass restore on any device signed in with the same Apple Account. Gems are consumable, so like coins they belong to the device they were bought on. For refunds, see Apple's refund page.

Can I turn off the video offers?

Yes. Go to Settings → Videos → Optional video offers. Videos never play unless you tap a button that says so, and the game is fully playable without them. The No-Video Pass makes every video reward instant.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and included in your normal iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes its progress.
